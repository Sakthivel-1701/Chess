This famous game was played in the 1959 Candidates Tournament when Fischer was only 16 years old and Tal was 23. Tal went on to win the tournament and earn the right to challenge Botvinnik for the World Championship. The game showcases Tal's aggressive, sacrificial style against Fischer's precise technique.
